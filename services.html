Armed Escort & Specialised Protection

Romanien Protection Limited recognises that certain approved movements and operational environments may require additional protective arrangements. Our proposed armed escort capability is based on lawful authorisation, qualified personnel, risk assessment and responsible security management.

  • Legally authorised armed escort services
  • Executive and VIP protective escort

  • Valuable asset movement protection
